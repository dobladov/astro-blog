            - 8 tbsp fried tomato sauce
        - 200 g chorizo, chopped
    - 400 g lentils
    - 1.5 l hot water
    - 2 potatoes, peeled and chopped
    - 1 pinch salt, to taste
method: 
  - Heat the extra virgin olive oil in a pot over medium-high heat.
  - Add the minced garlic, chopped onion, chopped leek, and a pinch of salt, then sauté for a couple of minutes.
  - Incorporate the chopped red bell pepper, green bell pepper, and carrots, and continue to sauté until the vegetables are soft and well-poached.
  - Stir in the bay leaves, cumin, sweet paprika, and fried tomato sauce.
  - Add the chopped chorizo and sauté briefly so it releases its flavor and integrates with the vegetable base.
  - Add the soaked lentils, hot water, and chopped potatoes, then season with additional salt if needed.
  - Cover the pot and simmer over medium heat for about 1 hour, shaking the pot occasionally instead of stirring too vigorously, until the lentils are tender and the stew has thickened.
notes: 
  - I like to add a splash of vinegar right before serving, for a more punchy flavour.
  - Ideal for meal prep as it yields many servings and can be frozen easily.
